WeaponsX - new weapons for Scrap Mechanic Survival
==================================================

Made for Scrap Mechanic 1.0.7a (Steam, Windows 64-bit).

REQUIRES SMLoader 1.5.0 or newer: https://github.com/CazymirTM/SMLoader
CO-OP: EVERY player in the world needs SMLoader + WeaponsX.
       (Co-op has not been tested yet.)

INSTALL
-------
1. Install SMLoader 1.5.0 or newer first (see its README).
2. Open your game folder:
   Steam -> right-click Scrap Mechanic -> Manage -> Browse local files.
3. Copy the  SMLoader  folder from this zip into the game folder and allow it
   to merge with the existing SMLoader folder. It adds:
     SMLoader\mods\WeaponsX.dll
4. Load a Survival world and look in any Craftbot.

The first time you take the Bazooka out it can be white for a few seconds,
while the game converts its textures.

USE
---
- Bazooka: 50 Metal Block Level 3, 15 Component Kit, 10 Circuit Board,
  10 Cable Roll.
- Rocket x2: 10 Metal Block Level 2, 2 Circuit Board, 5 Gasoline, 4 Ember.
- Left mouse fires, right mouse aims. It reloads by itself (2.5 seconds) while
  you have Rockets. The blast also hurts you and breaks blocks.

GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Back up your world before trying any mod.

UNINSTALL
---------
Delete  SMLoader\mods\WeaponsX.dll
A Bazooka and Rockets in your inventory are hidden, and come back if you
reinstall.
