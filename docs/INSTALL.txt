WeaponsX - new weapons for Scrap Mechanic Survival
==================================================

Made for Scrap Mechanic 1.0.7a (Steam, Windows 64-bit).

REQUIRES SMLoader 1.5.0 or newer: https://github.com/CazymirTM/SMLoader
CO-OP: EVERY player in the world needs SMLoader + WeaponsX.
       (Co-op has not been tested yet.)

INSTALL
-------
1. Install SMLoader 1.5.0 or newer first (see its README).
2. Open your game folder:
   Steam -> right-click Scrap Mechanic -> Manage -> Browse local files.
3. Copy the  SMLoader  folder from this zip into the game folder and allow it
   to merge with the existing SMLoader folder. It adds:
     SMLoader\mods\WeaponsX.dll
4. Load a Survival world and look in any Craftbot.

Updating from an older version: just replace WeaponsX.dll. In co-op everyone needs the same
version.

The first time you take a weapon out it can be white for a few seconds, while
the game converts its textures.

USE
---
- Bazooka: 50 Metal Block Level 3, 15 Component Kit, 10 Circuit Board,
  10 Cable Roll.
- Rocket x2: 10 Metal Block Level 2, 2 Circuit Board, 5 Gasoline, 4 Ember.
- Gravity Smasher: 50 Metal Block Level 3, 15 Component Kit, 15 Circuit Board,
  20 Battery.
- Gravity Balls x5: 15 Metal Block Level 2, 3 Circuit Board, 10 Battery, 5 Glow.
- Energy Sword: 35 Metal Block Level 3, 10 Component Kit, 10 Circuit Board,
  15 Battery.
- Energy Flux x18: 10 Metal Block Level 2, 2 Circuit Board, 6 Battery, 3 Glow.
- Gjadir: 75 Metal Block Level 3, 20 Component Kit, 20 Circuit Board,
  30 Battery.
- Infinity Fork: 40 Metal Block Level 3, 10 Component Kit, 10 Circuit Board,
  10 Battery.
- Bazooka and Gravity Smasher: left mouse fires, right mouse aims, R reloads the
  Gravity Smasher. Both reload by themselves when empty. The blasts also hurt
  you and break blocks.
- Gravity Smasher: hit a robot and it pulls all robots within 16 m together,
  then blows up. A miss does nothing. Its ammo is shown at the lower right.
- Energy Sword:
    Left mouse    slash, a combo of three that hits every robot in front of you
    Right mouse   energy wave, cuts through robots up to 34 m away. Costs one
                  Energy Flux. The faster you fire, the stronger they get. After
                  18 waves it recharges for 30 s (R recharges it earlier).
    Hold Q        aim a teleport, up to 14 m. Let go to teleport, right mouse
                  to cancel. 13 s cooldown.
    Jump          far higher while you hold the sword, every 8 s. You take no
                  fall damage, and a fall of 4 m or more smashes the ground.
- Gjadir (100 mana, refills by itself):
    Left mouse    five hits; the last one calls down thunder (15 mana)
    Hold right    aim a throw, let go to throw (12 mana)
    R             call the thrown hammer back (only with its slot selected)
    Hold R        lightning that chains through up to 8 robots
    Hold F        aim a lightning strike from the sky, let go to strike
                  (25 mana, 15 s cooldown)
    Hold Q        charge mana from the sky (12 s cooldown)
    Tap Q         Bifrost: travel to a waystone (40 mana, 60 s cooldown).
                  Needs the SMWaystones mod.
    Space in air  fly for up to 12 s (9 s cooldown). W A S D, Space up,
                  Ctrl down, Shift faster, Space twice to stop.
    Hammer away   left mouse: thunderclap, right mouse: stomp (4 s cooldown)
    Robots it destroys make it stronger and brighter. That fades 3 in-game
    days after the first one.
    The throw and the lightning strike break blocks, trees and crates, YOUR
    OWN BUILDINGS TOO.
- Infinity Fork:
    Left mouse    hits a little harder than a Sledgehammer and picks up all
                  items within 8 m
    Right mouse   block; raise it right before a hit to parry (no damage, the
                  robot is stunned for 5 s)
    Tap Q         plant seeds: pick a seed and how many, it plants the free
                  plots within 10 m
    Hold Q        Power Crop: 20 soil bags, 30 tomato seeds and two Haybot
                  bodyguards for 15 minutes (15 min cooldown)
    F             Ice Aura for 30 s: hits freeze robots (60 s cooldown)
    Tap R         Pump Up: swing about twice as fast for 7 s (16 s cooldown)
    Hold R        Ironbark for 60 s: +100 health and 20% armor (7 min cooldown)

GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Back up your world before trying any mod.

UNINSTALL
---------
Delete  SMLoader\mods\WeaponsX.dll
The weapons and their ammo in your inventory are hidden, and come back if you
reinstall. Do it while no bodyguards are out and Ironbark is off.
