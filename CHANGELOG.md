# Changelog

## 1.5 (2026-11-26)

- New weapon: the **Blades Of Zavir**, two burning blades on chains. A nine-hit
  combo that ends in a whirl into the ground, a hook that pulls you to a robot,
  **Hyperion Spin**, **Hell Teleport** through the ground, **Fire Aura** that
  sets robots on fire, and **Whiplash**, a wall of flames. Robots they destroy
  make them hotter and stronger, like Gjadir.
- **Gjadir, the Infinity Fork and the Blades Of Zavir are not crafted**, they
  have to be found:
  - Gjadir lies in about 3 % of Legendary and 0.3 % of Epic loot crates; lightning
    strikes the crate when it drops.
  - The Infinity Fork is dropped by about 7 % of Farmbots and 1 in 200 Haybots.
  - The Blades Of Zavir lie only in warehouses: in 1 of 10 reward chests, or in
    a loot crate there.
  - Each find has its own burst of light or fire and a message.
  - Weapons you already have stay yours.

## 1.4 (2026-11-26)

- New weapon: the **Infinity Fork**, a farmer's fork for fighting and farming.
- Every swing **picks up all items around you**, also what you just smashed out
  of a crate.
- **Block**, and **parry** at the right moment: no damage, and the robot is
  stunned for 5 seconds.
- **Plant Seeds**: pick a seed and how many, and it plants the free plots around
  you, grow beds too.
- **Power Crop**: soil bags, tomato seeds and two glowing Haybot **bodyguards**
  that follow you and fight for you.
- **Ice Aura** freezes robots, **Pump Up** makes you swing about twice as fast,
  **Ironbark** doubles your health and gives a little armor for a minute.
- Own model, icon, sounds, effects and first- and third-person animations.
- Fixed: a harmless error when a robot is destroyed in the moment it is thrown.

## 1.3 (2026-11-25)

- New weapon: **Gjadir**, the storm hammer. Five-hit combo that ends in a
  thunder strike, a throw you aim with an arrow, and a call that brings it back
  from anywhere.
- It runs on **mana** instead of ammo. Charge it with lightning from the sky.
- **Lightning strike** from the sky where the arrow points, and a **lightning
  chain** that jumps from robot to robot.
- **Flight**, and a whirling hammer while you hover.
- **Bifrost**: travel to the waystones of SMWaystones.
- **Bare hands** while the hammer is away: a thunderclap and a stomp.
- Robots it destroys make it stronger and brighter, for three in-game days.
- The throw and the lightning strike break blocks, trees and crates.
- Own model, icon, sounds, effects and first- and third-person animations.

## 1.2 (2026-11-25)

- New weapon: the **Energy Sword**. Slash with the left mouse button, send out
  energy waves that cut through robots with the right one. The faster you fire
  them, the stronger they get; a clip of 18 **Energy Flux** recharges in 30
  seconds.
- Hold Q to aim a teleport: an arrow shows where you will land.
- High jump while you hold the sword, without fall damage. From 4 m and up the
  landing smashes the ground and everything around you.
- Own model, icons, sounds, effects and first- and third-person animations, and
  an on-screen panel for ammo and cooldowns.

## 1.1 (2026-11-25)

- New weapon: the **Gravity Smasher**, a shotgun with a clip of five **Gravity
  Balls**. Hit a robot and a black hole pulls every robot around it together,
  then everything blows up. A miss does nothing.
- Own model, icons, sounds, glow, black hole and explosion effects, and own
  holding, shooting, pump and reload animations, in first and third person.
- Ammo counter on the screen for the Gravity Smasher.
- Fixed: reloading the Bazooka while sprinting skipped the hand animation.

## 1.0 (2026-11-24)

- First weapon: the **Bazooka**, with **Rockets** as its ammo. Both are in the
  Craftbot from the start.
- Own models, icons, sounds, explosion and smoke effects.
- Own shoulder-holding and reload animations, in first and third person.
