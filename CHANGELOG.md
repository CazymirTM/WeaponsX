# Changelog

## 1.1 (2026-11-25)

- New weapon: the **Gravity Smasher**, a shotgun with a clip of five **Gravity
  Balls**. Hit a robot and a black hole pulls every robot around it together,
  then everything blows up. A miss does nothing.
- Own model, icons, sounds, glow, black hole and explosion effects, and own
  holding, shooting, pump and reload animations, in first and third person.
- Ammo counter on the screen for the Gravity Smasher.
- Fixed: reloading the Bazooka while sprinting skipped the hand animation.

## 1.0 (2026-11-24)

- First weapon: the **Bazooka**, with **Rockets** as its ammo. Both are in the
  Craftbot from the start.
- Own models, icons, sounds, explosion and smoke effects.
- Own shoulder-holding and reload animations, in first and third person.
